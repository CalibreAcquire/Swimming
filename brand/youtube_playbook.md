Rules I apply as the editor (until Jake decides otherwise on the open points in the doc):

- Every YouTube publish pack: 10 title options (title case, under about 60 characters, plain words, no jargon unless the
  video is aimed at experienced investors), title and thumbnail text that complement each other and never repeat.
- Three thumbnails per video that are different concepts (not colour swaps), for YouTube's Test & Compare.
- A thumbnail brief (3 to 5 concepts) before filming where Jake shares the script in advance; thumbnails of Jake
  caught mid-sentence with a short line beside him ("click to unpause"), not posed.
- Ideas: when asked for topics, research outliers (3x+ a channel's average) in UK property/commercial and adjacent
  niches, cut 50+ ideas to 10 against set criteria, one-page slide each (title options, thumbnail sketch, outline, log line).
- Scripts: flag references to earlier videos ("as you saw last week") that lose new viewers.
- An expected rank (1 to 10 against the last ten videos) in each publish pack.
- Intro check: the title's promise shown or said in the first 10 seconds; flag openings over 45 seconds and topic lists.
- Flag ending language ("at the end of the day") mid-video.

Open decisions for Jake: brand tone vs curiosity titles, the script's intro structure, reach vs qualified audience,
the Dubai series' link to the channel's through line.
