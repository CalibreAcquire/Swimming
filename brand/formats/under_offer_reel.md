# Format: "Under offer" reel (deal agreed)

Jake's idea, 25 Nov 2026: whenever we agree a sale (under offer / sold subject to contract /
exchanged), post a short reel announcing it. Reference: a screen recording Jake saved of a
housebuilder agent's SSTC reel (we copy the format only, never their footage or branding).

## What the reference does (measured)
- 12-13 s, portrait, music only (no voice), no captions.
- 13 shots, a cut every 0.9 s on the beat: exterior hero, front door, hallway, living
  room, kitchen details, staircase, bedroom, bathroom, then a drone aerial.
- Text: only two moments. The development name over the opening exterior (large, light
  serif, white, centred), and the agent's logo over the closing aerial. A small logo mark
  on the front door shot. The status ("SSTC") is in the post caption, not on screen.
- Clean, bright, slightly warm grade; smooth gimbal/drone movement, no zoom effects.

## Our version
- 10-15 s, 9:16 1080x1920, music from `brand/music/` (upbeat, steady), cuts on the beat
  (about 0.8-1.0 s each), 10-14 shots of the building's own footage (Kim's shoot or ours).
- Opening: best exterior with the address in Cormorant Garamond (e.g. "479 Hornsey Road"),
  white, centred, a small spaced label above it with the status ("UNDER OFFER" or "SOLD").
- Close: drone/wide exterior with the Calibre Acquire wordmark (white) centred.
- Small "C." monogram on one detail shot (the door), like the reference's logo mark.
- No voice, no captions, no prices on screen. Status wording must match the deal
  exactly and Jake confirms it each time (under offer / sold STC / exchanged / completed).
- Listing rules apply: never "off-market"; no buyer details.
- Caption: one line of status + address, two or three factual bullets (type of building,
  units/area), the register line and URL. Comment keyword only if a campaign applies.
- Posts: Instagram reel + YouTube Short + TikTok, same file and cover.

## Trigger
Any time Jake says a property is agreed/under offer, or the monday board / diary shows it,
offer this reel. Needs: the building's walkthrough and drone footage, and the status wording.
