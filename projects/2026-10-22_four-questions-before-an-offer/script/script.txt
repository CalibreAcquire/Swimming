[QUESTION 2 — TRUE COST]
What does this actually cost beyond the purchase price?
(pause)
Service charge, ground rent, repairs, planning constraints — these don't show up in the headline figure,
but they affect your return every year you hold the property.
Ask for the last three years of service charge accounts if there's one in place.
Ask whether any major works are planned or already agreed.
A property that looks cheaper on paper can end up costing more once these are factored in.
[GRAPHIC: "Question 2 — What does this actually cost beyond the purchase price?"]
[QUESTION 3 — VENDOR MOTIVATION]
Why is the vendor actually selling?
(pause)
This shapes how the negotiation goes and what you might uncover.
A vendor selling because of probate, retirement, or a change in circumstances behaves differently to one
selling because the property has become a problem.
It's not always something you'll get a straight answer to, but it's worth asking your agent or solicitor what
they know.
The reason behind a sale often explains things the listing itself won't.
[GRAPHIC: "Question 3 — Why is the vendor actually selling?"]
[QUESTION 4 — EXIT STRATEGY]
Who would buy this from me later?
(pause)
Before you buy, it's worth knowing what your future buyer looks like.
A property with broad appeal — good location, flexible use, no unusual lease structure — is easier to sell
on.
A highly specific asset can be harder to move, even at a fair price.
This doesn't mean you need to plan an exit on day one.
But if you can't picture who'd want this property in five or ten years, that's worth considering before you
offer.
[GRAPHIC: "Question 4 — Who would buy this from me later?"]
