# Why buy an office or shop at Binghatti in Al Jaddaf: the evidence

Sources in Drive › Binghatti – Investor Data: `AlJaddaf-JVC_sales_12m_Bayut-DLD-summary.csv`, `AlJaddaf-JVC_rents_12m_Bayut-DLD-summary.csv`, `CultureVillage_12m_Bayut-DLD-summary.csv` (registered sales and rents, 12 months to 22-25 Oct 2026), the Binghatti live feed and one-page summary (22 Oct 2026), the Q3 2026 Dubai office report figures in the investor data sheet. On screen and in copy: say "registered sales" or "the last 12 months", never name the data source.

Changes are Bayut's 12-month comparisons of average price or rent per sq ft. They mix whatever sold in each period, so small samples swing a lot; the sample size is next to each figure.

## Capital growth (price per sq ft, last 12 months)

Strong samples:
- Dubai offices: AED 2,763 per sq ft, **up 59%**, with sales **up 48%** (5,466 sales).
- Dubai commercial overall: AED 2,536 per sq ft, **up 48%**, sales **up 26%** (9,451 sales).
- JVC offices: AED 1,850, **up 34%** (175 sales); off-plan offices up 36% (130), ready up 23% (45).
- JVC shops: AED 2,634, **up 16%** (219 sales); off-plan up 18% (162).

Al Jaddaf itself (small samples, use with care):
- All commercial: AED 2,185, up 47% (52 sales; the ready ones include whole buildings).
- Off-plan commercial: up 86% (24 sales). Offices: AED 1,529, up 36% (19 sales). Shops: up 115% (5 sales: too few to quote).
- Culture Village (Twilight, Moonlight): office sales up 182% to 48, AED 2,497 per sq ft (up 2.3%).

## Rental growth (rent per sq ft, last 12 months)

- **Al Jaddaf shops: AED 173 per sq ft, up 19%; average rent up 17%** (341 contracts). The strongest local figure.
- Al Jaddaf commercial overall: rent per sq ft up 34% (506 contracts).
- JVC shops: AED 201, up 9.6%, average rent up 20% (617 contracts). JVC offices: AED 99, up 17%, contracts up 40% (345).
- Culture Village offices: AED 84, up 16% (33 contracts).
- Dubai offices: 238,712 contracts, up 10%; AED 107 per sq ft.

## Spectre itself

- 128 of 230 offices sold since the 3 September launch (in about seven weeks); 12 of 19 shops sold.
- Offices are listed at AED 2,624 per sq ft against AED 2,639 already paid: no discounting.
- **The 7 remaining shops average AED 3,687 per sq ft, about 4% below the AED 3,841 earlier buyers paid.**
- 19 shops on the ground floor below 712 apartments and 235 offices (around 1,500 residents, our estimate).
- Two-thirds of Dubai office lettings in Q3 2026 were units under 1,000 sq ft; Spectre's offices start at 705 sq ft.
- Location (Jake's one-pager): on Dubai Creek, 10 to 15 minutes from Downtown, Business Bay and the airport, two metro stations open; next to DEWA's headquarters.

## Be straight about income

At like-for-like local office rents (AED 84 to 99 per sq ft), a Spectre office returns about 3% to 4% gross at the asking price; shops about 3% to 5%. The case is capital growth, location and owner-occupation to a 2028 handover, not yield (as Jake's investor one-pager already says). Don't put a yield on screen for Spectre.
