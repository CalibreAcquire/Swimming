```

- For tax purposes, a sale usually happens when contracts are exchanged, not when it completes.
```

```
Capital gains tax at 40%? The numbers.

- There was talk of bringing capital gains tax in line with income tax, taking the higher rate on property gains from 24% to 40% or more.
- The Chancellor is reported to have rejected that. Reported, not announced.
- A higher-rate landlord with a £200,000 gain pays about £47,000 today, after the £3,000 allowance. At 40% it would have been nearly £79,000.
```

### Reel 5 (Making Tax Digital)
```
Making Tax Digital: the threshold drops to £30,000 from April 2027.

- Since April this year, landlords with combined rental and self-employed income over £50,000 have had to keep digital records and report to HMRC every quarter.
- From April 2027, that threshold drops to £30,000.
- It's based on your income before expenses, not your profit.

Save this and check whether it applies to you.
```
