### 3rd: Reel 3 (Two traps)
```
Two traps that make advertised yields look better than they are.

- Asking rent, not the rent being paid. A flat marketed at £1,400 a month that's actually let at £1,250 has a lower yield than the advert suggests. Ask for the tenancy agreements and proof the rent is being paid.
- Rents that include bills. HMO rooms are often let with bills included, so the rent looks higher, but the landlord pays the council tax, utilities and wifi.
```

```
Before you make an offer, ask for these.

- The service charge accounts for the last three years.
- Any notices of planned major works.
- The insurance schedule.
- The tenancy agreements and rent statements.
- On a freehold, a survey that tells you what the building will need in the next five years.
```
