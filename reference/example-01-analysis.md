# Reference 01 — Instagram ad (personal-branding coach)

Source: screen recording on Google Drive, `ScreenRecording_10-21-2026 14-19-13_1.mp4`.
9:16 reel, ~73s of speech (0:02–0:75 of the recording). Not committed — video
files stay on Drive.

## Measurements
| Metric                 | Value                                              |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Speaking pace          | 273 words in 73s ≈ **224 words/min** (very fast)   |
| Longest pause kept     | **0.44s** — every gap is cut out                   |
| Visual change          | every **~2s** (shot change, B-roll or text card)   |
| Loudness               | **−12.4 LUFS** integrated (loud, phone-first mix)  |

## Script structure
| Time      | Beat        | Content                                                  |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| 0–7s      | Hook        | "You need a personal brand that's going to turn that chase into a queue of high quality leads." |
| 7–18s     | Problem     | Ads, cold outreach, networking; "starting from zero" each month |
| 18–30s    | Solution    | Personal brand → trust over time → "stop chasing, start choosing" |
| 30–52s    | Proof/claim | "$20,000 a month", gym analogy, triple your income        |
| 52–67s    | Credibility | "$6 million", name, client names with their profiles      |
| 67–75s    | CTA         | Book a free call, "Links below."                          |

## Visual techniques
1. **Word-by-word stacked captions** (the signature). One to three words on
   screen, built up in tiers in the lower-middle of the frame:
   - small *italic serif* (connective words: "turn", "starting", "Building")
   - medium bold white sans ("that", "from", "a")
   - huge heavy caps in gold (the key word: "CHASE", "ZERO", "PERSONAL BRAND")
   Some key words are italic serif instead ("networking", "dynamic", "business").
2. **Numbers and names as key words** — "$20,000", "$6 MILLION", "ONE",
   client names over their Instagram profiles.
3. **Location changes** — kitchen, desk set-up, close-up selfie. Each new
   section of the script gets a new setting.
4. **B-roll every few seconds** — gym, graduation, typing, screen recordings of
   DMs, revenue dashboard, client profiles.
5. **Full-screen plain cards** — light grey background, dark word-by-word text,
   with simple 2D illustrations (money in hands, person at laptop).
6. **Proof overlays** — revenue dashboard floated over the talking shot.
7. **CTA format switch** — for the last 8s the caption style changes to
   sentence chunks in black boxes at the top, over a warm close-up. This signals
   "this is the ask".
8. **Grade** — muted, slightly desaturated, soft contrast; warm on the CTA.
