# Video Ideas: December 2026

The first monthly ideas list from the YouTube Playbook's action plan. I wrote 52 ideas from outlier videos and our own best performers, cut them to 10 against five criteria, and wrote a one-page plan for each. Jake picks one (or two) to script next.

## How this list was made

- **Outlier scan, 26 November 2026.** I looked at the latest 60 long-form videos from 63 channels: 51 in UK property and 12 in adjacent niches (US commercial property, buying a business, Dubai, London, economics). Each video was scored as its views ÷ the channel's median views. 563 videos scored 3x or more. The full list is in `research/ideas/2026-12/outliers.json` in the repo, made with `scripts/outliers.py`.
- **Our own channel.** I compared every long-form video and Short against our own median (about 20 views for long form).
- **Five cut criteria:**
  1. **Filmable:** Jake can film it with our own deals, buildings and knowledge, without stock or other people's footage.
  2. **Packageable:** it works as a title and thumbnail you can read at a glance.
  3. **Can beat our average:** the target is 500+ views in 28 days, Holloway level.
  4. **Through line:** London commercial and mixed-use buying and selling, or the Dubai campaign.
  5. **Brand and content rules:** factual tone, no naming companies, nothing we can't verify.

Names of channels and companies below are for our reference only; none go into public content.

## What already works for us

- **Long form:** "£300k–£400k Below Break-Up Value | Holloway, London, N19" has 1,700 views, about 85x our median. Nothing else passes 66. What it had that the others didn't:
  - an exact figure in the title
  - a price-below-value promise
  - a real place
- **Shorts:** our best performers are about tax timing and specific buildings:
  - "Selling before the Budget? …" (889)
  - "The biggest landlord tax change won't be in the Budget" (721)
  - "A private viewing: Binghatti, Al Jaddaf" (473)
  - "Capital gains tax at 40%? The real numbers" (472)
  - "Brompton freehold: £90,320 a year…" (381)
- **Letters** (published 23 Nov) has 7 views on the title currently showing. It's early, but it's tracking below the expectation we'd set for it.
- **Pattern:** a real building plus an exact figure, and tax with a deadline. Generic "how to" titles ("Top Three Property Strategies in 2026", 18 views) don't travel.

## The shortlist: ten one-page plans

Each plan has:

- a one-line summary
- title options
- a thumbnail sketch
- the video's outline
- the stakes
- the facts to verify
- an expected rank against our last ten videos (1 = best)

Full sets of ten titles get written once Jake picks the idea.

### 1. Everyone's Buying Flats. Here's What We Buy Instead.

- **One line:** why we buy whole freehold buildings with shops below and homes above, instead of single flats, shown on a real building.
- **Titles:**
  - Everyone's Buying Flats. Here's What We Buy Instead.
  - Why We Buy the Whole Building, Not the Flat
  - Five Flats or One Freehold: Same Money, Different Result
- **Thumbnail:** Jake mid-sentence in front of a Victorian parade with flats above, hand towards the building; text "THE WHOLE BUILDING".
- **Outline:**
  1. Hook: this building pays £90,320 a year (Brompton).
  2. What the same money buys in flats.
  3. Four reasons (the freehold, two incomes, tenants who repair, site value).
  4. The honest downsides (an empty shop, finance).
  5. Who it suits.
  6. Register CTA.
- **Stakes:** what a leasehold flat's service charge and lease length cost over ten years.
- **Verify:** the Brompton figures, and any flat comparison figures (from Jake).
- **Expected rank:** 1 of 10.
